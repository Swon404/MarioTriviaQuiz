# Progression pacing for review

Approved by Dad on 29 October; implementation is the next stage. These are
arithmetic scenarios, not observed family earnings.
Lifetime points remain the actual points earned in games, even if Championship
standings later award one point per game won. No bonus currency or multipliers.

| Completed activity | Possible points for one person |
| --- | --- |
| Quick Quiz | 0–3 |
| Three competitive timed Hunt rounds | 0–3 |
| Three solo Game Order boards | 3 |
| Two Rookie Clue Match boards | 0–8 |
| Untimed shared Hunt | Variable: one per pair actually found |

At three points per completed session, the first current rank at 50 takes
17 sessions, the 500-point milestone 167, and the 2,000-point milestone 667.
At eight points per session those take 7, 63 and 250 sessions. Matching therefore
advances ranks faster than the family's other favourite, Game Order.

Proposal: keep all six rank names, lower thresholds from **0/50/150/300/500/800**
to **0/20/60/120/240/400**. Change Rising Star from **500 to 100** and Super Star
from **2,000 to 500**. At three points per session, the first rank takes seven
sessions; the point milestones take 34 and 167. At eight they take three,
13 and 63. These are cumulative rewards, not targets or deadlines.

All existing points and other achievements stay. Lower thresholds preserve
every already-earned rank/milestone and may unlock a higher rank immediately.
No reset, popup, streak pressure or daily requirement. We can retain the current
thresholds if the family prefers a longer climb. Actual pacing still needs family play.
