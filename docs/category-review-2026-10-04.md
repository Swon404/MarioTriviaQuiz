# Category Finder continuation

Reviewed against [Nintendo's US Mario history timeline](https://www.nintendo.com/us/explore/characters/mario/history/) on 4 October 2026. Six additions use its release dates and original platforms: Super Mario Land, Super Mario Land 2, New Super Mario Bros., Super Mario 3D Land, Super Mario Galaxy 2 and Super Mario Maker 2.

The catalogue now contains 46 entities. Its original 40 positions and IDs remain unchanged so existing window history stays meaningful; the additions follow them.

Four contextual groups supplement the five original types for Pro and Legend: before 2000, original Wii release, original Switch release, and original Game Boy/DS/3DS release. Rookie still asks the original type-recognition questions. Prompts and context specify original releases, excluding later ports or collections. A title can fit multiple groups: Super Mario Land is a game, a pre-2000 release and an original handheld release. Bot selection, answer validation and displayed correct tiles use the same membership function.

Every generated board has a valid choice and at least one distractor. All existing board dimensions, consecutive-window rules, scoring and turn rules remain unchanged. New browser coverage selects a handheld game from a forced contextual window and checks other valid tiles are also marked correct. Legend phone text is larger than before and the full board remains five columns with no document overflow, though some long words still wrap. Physical-device text scaling and family readability remain outstanding.
