# Three short family acceptance sessions

Not yet performed. Browser emulation does not fill in these results.
Record iPhone model, iOS version, Safari or Home Screen, app build and text size.
Ask Dad and Blaize separately what was confusing and what they want to play again.

1. **Favourite games:** two-player Hunt and Game Order, three consecutive rounds.
   Try 16/20 pairs and eight Order tiles. Check recognition, readable names,
   timer/winner clarity, personal bests and Top 5. Watch both replays and Stop.
2. **Championship:** a short run including timed play. Each person explains who
   won and why. Lock/switch apps once while timing, reload after a completed go,
   and check the frozen time, active player and carried totals. Note actual
   per-game earnings before accepting balance or progression changes.
3. **Shroomer and discovery:** play the preferred level, watch its moves and try
   a new fact in Learning Zone. Note excessive waiting or unfair-looking memory.
   Let a newcomer start and exit a game using the optional help.

In short follow-ups, cover other modes, portrait/landscape, expanded browser bars,
larger text, voice/silent play and reduced motion. Install to Home Screen, revisit
offline, then try backup download/import through Files. Check update prompts
between games. Do not discard the backup after testing.

Include a brief VoiceOver pass: controls should identify their purpose, hidden
cards must not speak their answers, and changing rounds/exiting should stop old
speech. Try accidental double taps on answer, Check, Next, restart, exit, replay
and settings controls. Confirm no extra points, skipped rounds or leftover bot moves.

For any missing score, record player, score type, exact URL, Safari/Home Screen
context, build, before/after restart steps and any storage warning. Keep existing
data; do not clear the site as a first troubleshooting step. That report is still
unresolved and is not explained merely by removing old record caps.

Acceptance requires no known score-loss, missing-target, stuck/skipped-turn or
hidden-answer fault; independent play; readable controls; useful discoveries;
and both players voluntarily choosing another go. Test counts cannot establish
those last judgements. Log failures in the main plan for repair and retest.
