# Game Order source review

Checked 4 October 2026 against [Nintendo's US Mario history timeline](https://www.nintendo.com/us/explore/characters/mario/history/).

The game follows this published timeline rather than claiming earliest worldwide release dates. This distinction matters for games released in different years across regions. The rule is shown before a puzzle starts. Years remain hidden until it is solved.

The pool expands from 11 to 22 titles. Additions: Super Mario Bros. 2 (1988), Super Mario Land 2 (1992), New Super Mario Bros. (2006), New Super Mario Bros. Wii (2009), Super Mario 3D Land (2011), New Super Mario Bros. 2 (2012), Super Mario Maker (2015), Super Mario Run (2016), Super Mario Maker 2 (2019), Super Mario 3D All-Stars (2020), and Super Mario 3D World + Bowser’s Fury (2021).

Only one title per year is included, avoiding hidden month/day tie-breakers. Timings now use `order-v2`; older results stay available in separate Legacy tables. Restarted boards are practice and cannot replace the first saved completion. Scoring, penalties and tile-count options are unchanged.
